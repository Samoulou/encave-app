About this experience

Immerse yourself in the heart of the Valais vineyards with our Heritage Tasting & Cellar Tour. This exclusive experience begins with a guided walk through our historic terraced vines, where you will learn about the unique microclimate and soil composition that gives our wines their distinctive character.

Following the vineyard walk, we invite you into our century-old cellar. Here, surrounded by oak barrels, our sommelier will guide you through a tasting of 5 signature wines, including our award-winning Petite Arvine and Syrah. The tasting is accompanied by a platter of local Valais cheeses and cured meats.
